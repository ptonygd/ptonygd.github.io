Understanding Your Numbers

Strain
0 – 21
How hard today actually was. Built from session volume, intensity relative to your own bests, and the RPE you logged, then compressed onto a 0–21 scale so a genuinely brutal day still has somewhere to go. Two days at 14 are twice the work of two days at 7 only in feel — the scale is deliberately non-linear at the top.
Recovery
0 – 100%
How ready your body says it is. Sleep duration and quality, soreness, energy, resting heart rate and HRV each contribute, weighted against your own rolling baseline rather than a population average. Your 100% is yours.
Load Ratio (ACWR)
0.8 – 1.3 is the sweet spot
This week's training load divided by your rolling four-week average. Above 1.3 you're ramping faster than you've adapted to; below 0.8 you're losing ground. Sports-science literature ties sustained excursions above the band to raised soft-tissue injury risk.
Monotony
Lower is better
Foster's measure of how same-y your week is. Identical effort every day scores high and predicts staleness even when total volume is sensible. Hard days genuinely hard, easy days genuinely easy — that's what brings it down.
Estimated 1RM
Epley formula
Your best single, estimated from a set you actually did. Accurate through about 8 reps and increasingly optimistic beyond that — treat a 15-rep e1RM as a rough direction, not a number to load the bar with.

None of this is medical advice. Tony Fitter is a training tool, not a clinician. If something hurts in a way that training soreness doesn't, see a professional.

My numbers don't match what I see in AdMob or AdSense

Almost always time zone. Today shows zero, or far less than expected

Google's reporting runs behind live traffic — usually a couple of hours, occasionally longer. Early in your account's day there may genuinely be nothing reported yet. Pull to refresh later rather than treating an empty morning as a fault.

Sign-in fails, or the permission screen won't complete

Ads Earning asks only for read-only reporting access. If the screen closes without returning, check that you're picking the Google account that actually owns the publisher account — a personal account with no AdMob or AdSense attached will authorise fine and then show nothing.

Only one of AdMob or AdSense is showing

They're separate products with separate publisher accounts. Ads Earning shows whichever the signed-in Google account has access to — if you only hold AdMob, only AdMob appears, and page views and page RPM stay hidden because AdMob doesn't report them.

Can it change anything in my Google account?

No. The permissions granted are read-only reporting scopes. There is no write access, so the App cannot create, edit, pause or delete an ad unit, change a payment setting, or alter anything at all in AdMob or AdSense. It can only read the same numbers you'd read yourself.

The widget is showing yesterday's number

iOS decides when widgets refresh and rations those refreshes to protect battery — the widget requests an update regularly but the system may defer it. Opening the app forces a fresh read and the widget catches up.

If it's stuck for a long stretch, check Settings → General → Background App Refresh is on and that Low Power Mode isn't active. Low Power Mode suspends widget updates almost entirely.

Which currency are the figures in?

Whatever currency your Google publisher account reports in. The App doesn't convert — converting would introduce a rate that doesn't match your actual payment, and the point is that the number matches what Google will pay you.

My unpaid balance doesn't match the payout progress

Unpaid earnings accumulate against the payment threshold set on your Google account, and only finalised earnings count toward it. The current month's estimate is shown alongside but isn't part of the balance until Google closes the month.

Sign out inside the App and the stored token is deleted from the iOS Keychain immediately. To revoke the grant on Google's side as well, remove Ads Earning at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the App does both by removing the Keychain entry along with it.

Sign out and back in with the other account to switch. Each sign-in replaces the stored token rather than accumulating them, so only one publisher account is loaded at a time.

FAQ

I went somewhere listed and they don't take bitcoin

Listings are community-sourced from BTC Map and OpenStreetMap, so they reflect what a contributor last recorded — which may be months old, and merchants change their minds. Check the verification date on the place before you travel; a listing verified recently is a much better bet than one that hasn't been touched in a year.

The fix belongs upstream. Correcting it at btcmap.org updates the data for every app that uses it, not just this one. Tell us as well if you'd like, but the upstream edit is what actually helps the next traveller.

How do I add a place that's missing?

Add it to BTC Map. Bitcoin Places doesn't hold its own copy of the directory — it reads the shared open dataset — so there's no in-app submission that would go anywhere useful. Contributing upstream puts your place in front of everyone using that data.

"Near me" isn't sorting anything

It needs location permission. Check Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Bitcoin Places, and make sure Precise Location is on — with it off, iOS supplies a coarse area and distance sorting gets vague.

Your location is used on your device to sort the list. It isn't uploaded, and the directory works fine without it if you'd rather browse by search or map.

"Open now" says open but the place is shut

Opening hours come from OpenStreetMap and are only as current as the last contributor made them. The status is computed in the venue's own local time, which is the right behaviour when you're travelling, but it can't know about public holidays, seasonal closures, or a sign on the door saying back in an hour.

Treat it as a strong hint, not a guarantee — particularly for anywhere you'd be annoyed to arrive at and find closed.

Does it work without a connection?

Yes, once an area has loaded. Places you've already browsed, your Passport and the map region stay available offline — useful when you land somewhere without a local SIM.

What needs a connection: pulling in an area you haven't loaded yet, and refreshing verification dates. Directions hand off to Apple Maps, which has its own offline behaviour.

My saved places disappeared after reinstalling

Your Passport is stored on your device and nowhere else — there's no account, so there's no server copy to restore from. Deleting the app removes it.

That's the trade for having no sign-up and no data leaving your phone, but it does mean a reinstall or a new phone starts empty.

What's the difference between on-chain, Lightning and Lightning contactless?

See the Directory tab — each payment method is explained there, along with what to expect at the counter.

Can I pay through the app?

No. Bitcoin Places is a directory and nothing more. It doesn't process payments, hold funds, connect to a wallet, or take part in any transaction — it tells you where to go, and you pay with whatever wallet you already use.

The map is slow in a busy city

Dense areas are clustered so the map stays readable; zoom in to break a cluster apart. If it's genuinely sluggish rather than just crowded, tell us the city and zoom level and we'll take a look.

The app crashed — what should I do?

Force-quit, reopen, and try again. If the crash repeats, contact us with your device model, iOS version, and roughly where on the map you were — crashes tied to a specific area usually point at one malformed listing, and knowing the region is what lets us find it.

Before You Use a Bitcoin ATM

Bitcoin ATMs are convenient and legal, and they are also the single most common way people get defrauded out of cash they can't get back. Two facts matter more than anything else on this screen:

Fees are typically high. Operator margins on bitcoin ATMs are routinely far above what you'd pay at an exchange. Check the rate and fee shown on the machine before you commit, not after.

Transactions are irreversible. Once bitcoin leaves the machine there is no chargeback, no reversal, and no support line that can undo it. Unlike a card payment, nobody can claw it back for you.

🛑

The One Rule

Never put cash into a bitcoin ATM because someone told you to. Not a phone call, not a text, not an email, not someone you've only met online. If another person is directing you to a machine, you are being robbed — the fact that they sound calm, official or kind is the technique, not a reassurance.

Scammers reach people by impersonating something trustworthy. The story varies; the instruction never does.

  • Police, a court, immigration or a tax office claiming you owe money or face arrest
  • Your bank's "fraud team" saying your account is compromised and funds must be moved to a safe wallet
  • A utility, delivery firm or government agency demanding immediate payment
  • Tech support who found a problem on your computer
  • A romantic interest, or a friend on a hacked account, with an emergency
  • An investment or job opportunity that needs a deposit first

No government agency, bank, court or utility on earth will ever ask you to pay in bitcoin. There is no such thing as a safe wallet they need you to move money to. Anyone saying otherwise is stealing from you, however plausible they sound.

  • Stop. Urgency is manufactured — nothing genuine collapses because you took an hour
  • Hang up and call the organisation back on a number you looked up yourself, never one you were given
  • Tell someone you trust before you send anything, out loud. Scripts like this fall apart the moment they're said to a second person
  • If you've already sent funds, contact your local police and the ATM operator immediately — recovery is unlikely, but reporting it matters and you are not the first

Being taken in by one of these says nothing about your intelligence. They are professionally built, they target everyone, and the people running them do this full time.

金融機関の試算と金額が少し違います

本アプリは元利均等返済の標準的な計算式で算出しています。実際の金融機関の試算とわずかにずれる主な理由は、端数処理の方法（円未満の切り捨て・切り上げ）、初回返済までの日割り利息、そして融資手数料や保証料が計算に含まれていないことです。

数千円程度の差であれば、この端数処理と日割りの範囲内であることがほとんどです。正式な返済額は必ず金融機関の返済予定表でご確認ください。

「借入可能額」は審査に通る金額ですか

いいえ。毎月の返済予算から数学的に逆算した金額であり、審査の結果とは別のものです。

実際の融資可能額は、年収・勤続年数・他の借入・信用情報・物件の担保評価など、アプリが知り得ない要素で決まります。予算感をつかむための目安としてお使いください。

返済負担率は何％までが安全ですか

アプリでは年収に対する年間返済額の割合を計算します。一般に25〜35％が一つの目安とされますが、基準は金融機関ごとに異なり、教育費や車の維持費といった各家庭の事情は反映されません。

数値が基準内だから安全、という判断材料ではなく、比較のための共通のものさしとしてご覧ください。

繰り上げ返済の「期間短縮」と「返済額軽減」はどちらが得ですか

同じ金額を繰り上げる場合、利息の軽減額は期間短縮型のほうが大きくなります。返済額軽減型は総利息の削減幅は小さいものの、毎月の負担が下がるため家計に余裕が生まれます。

アプリでは両方を並べて試算できますので、削減額の差と毎月の安心感を見比べてご判断ください。金融機関によっては手数料や最低金額の条件がある点にもご注意ください。

金利変更のシミュレーションはどういう前提ですか

指定した時点以降、新しい金利がそのまま続くという前提で再計算します。変動金利の将来を予測するものではありません。

「1％上がったら毎月いくら増えるか」を確かめるための道具としてお使いいただくのが適切です。複数のパターンを試して、上振れに耐えられるかを確認することをおすすめします。

仲介手数料の「速算式」と「区分計算」で答えが違います

いいえ、一致します。速算式は区分計算を簡略化したもので、売買価格が400万円を超える場合は同じ結果になります。400万円以下では区分ごとの計算が必要なため、アプリは正式な区分計算にも対応しています。

なお表示されるのは法定の上限額です。実際の手数料は不動産会社との合意で決まり、上限より低い場合もあります。別途消費税がかかります。

諸費用や税額が最新の制度と合っていない気がします

税率や軽減措置は改正されます。アプリは一般的な税率にもとづく概算であり、住宅の種類・築年数・床面積・取得時期などによる特例や軽減措置をすべて反映するものではありません。
